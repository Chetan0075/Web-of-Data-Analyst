How I Work

Same process, every time. That's the point.

01 / DEFINE

Ask the real question

Before a chart exists, I find out what decision it needs to support — and who's actually making it.

02 / MODEL

Build the data model

SQL and Python to clean and shape the data; DAX to encode the business logic correctly, once.

03 / VISUALIZE

Design the signal

A Power BI dashboard built to show what changed and why — not just what the numbers are today.

04 / ACT

Hand off a decision

Every dashboard ships with a point of view: here's what I'd do next, and why.
